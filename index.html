Our Legacy

Our Journey (2004 – 2026)

From a small two-room tuition madrasa with a tin roof to a growing school serving hundreds of students — our journey has been built through dedication, service, and community support.

2004

A Humble Beginning

Our journey began in 2004 with just 10 students. At that time, the school was a small two-room tuition madrasa with a simple tin roof. Resources were limited, but the vision was clear — to provide education to children who needed it.

Early Years

Growing With Determination

As more families began to trust the institution, the number of students gradually increased. What started with a few students in a small madrasa slowly began developing into a more organized educational institution.

Growth & Development

Building a Better Learning Environment

With continued support and dedication, the school began expanding its facilities. Additional classrooms were built to accommodate the growing number of students and to provide a better environment for learning.

A New Step Forward

Introducing School Uniforms

In the early days, students did not have a school uniform. As the institution developed, we introduced uniforms so that students could have a stronger sense of identity, equality, and belonging within the school community.

Expansion

From 2 Rooms to 12–13 Rooms

Over the years, the school continued to grow physically as well as academically. The original two-room setup has now developed into a school with approximately 12 to 13 rooms, allowing more students and classes to be accommodated.

2026

A School Serving 450 Students

Today, in 2026, our school has grown from just 10 students to 450 students receiving free education. From a humble two-room tin-roof madrasa to a school with approximately 12–13 rooms, this journey represents more than physical growth — it represents years of commitment to providing children with access to education.

Looking Ahead

Our Vision for the Future

Today, our school provides free education to 450 students from an underprivileged community where poverty and limited educational opportunities continue to affect access to learning.

As the number of children seeking education continues to grow, our existing space is becoming increasingly limited. We want to expand our school facilities so that we can welcome more children who are currently unable to access quality education.

Expanding Our Reach

Our immediate goal is to expand our classrooms and facilities and gradually increase our capacity from 450 students to 600 and eventually 700 students. This expansion will allow us to serve more children and provide them with a safe and supportive environment in which they can learn, grow, and build a better future.
